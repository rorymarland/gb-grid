    How these figures are made

    Nobody publishes each power station’s output live. The figures here start from what each generating unit told the grid operator it would do (its Physical Notification), adjusted by any instruction the operator has since given it, then every unit of the same fuel is scaled so that together they match Elexon’s measured total for that fuel. They are good estimates, not meter readings.

    History is kept for seven days. The capacity factor is the energy generated as a share of what the station would make running flat out for the same time.
