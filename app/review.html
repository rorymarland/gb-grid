      Your home’s year

          How this works

          The grid’s figures come from NESO’s historic generation mix, the same half hours as the History page, and Elexon’s wholesale prices. A year’s carbon intensity is weighted by how much was generated, so a busy winter evening counts for more than a quiet summer night; History’s yearly figures are a plain average of the half hours, so they can differ by a few g/kWh.

          While a year is still going, it runs to the last whole day NESO has published, usually two days ago, and compares with the same days of the year before.

          Wind turned down and constraint costs come from the Constraints page’s data, which goes back about 400 days from when the dashboard first ran. Moments come from the timeline, which keeps about 400 days too.

          Your home’s year uses the meter readings and prices the dashboard already keeps, with carbon at the grid’s intensity in each half hour and gas at 0.183 kg of CO₂ per kWh. Making this page sends no requests to Octopus.

          Two weeks after a year ends, once the late data is in, the page is saved as it stands and kept, so it reads the same in years to come.
